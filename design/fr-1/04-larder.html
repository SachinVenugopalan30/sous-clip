No. 0417 / Chinese / Steamed

Steamed Pork Dumplings
(Layered Wrapper Style)

20 min cook8 min prep4 servingsSaved today

Ingredients / 12

400 gground pork
4 clovesgarlic, grated
1 tbspginger, grated
3green onions
2 tbsplight soy sauce
1 tbspoyster sauce
1 tbspcornstarch
2 tbspwater
1 tspsalt
½ tspwhite pepper
24gyoza wrappers
to servechili oil
From the video

“Do not skip the wet wrapper. The water steams the layer from the inside and keeps it from cracking.”

Method / 9

1

Grate the garlic and ginger. Slice the green onions, keeping the white and green parts apart.

2

Combine the pork, garlic, ginger, onion whites, soy, oyster sauce, cornstarch, water, salt and white pepper.

2 min
3

Mix the filling until it is sticky and well combined. Fold it over itself rather than stirring in circles.

4

Place a wet gyoza wrapper in a ramekin. Add a spoon of filling, press a second wrapper on top, and repeat to build the layers.

8 min
5

Steam over rolling water for 12 minutes, until the wrapper turns translucent and pulls away cleanly.

12 min
6

Turn out onto a plate, spoon over the chili oil and finish with the green onion tops.
