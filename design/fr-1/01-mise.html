Steamed Pork Dumplings
(Layered Wrapper Style)

20 min cook 4 servings Steamed
ChinesePorkDumplingsWeekend

Ingredients

4 servings
400 gground pork
4 clovesgarlic, grated
1 tbspginger, grated
3green onions, sliced
2 tbsplight soy sauce
1 tbspoyster sauce
1 tbspcornstarch
2 tbspwater
1 tspsalt
½ tspwhite pepper
24gyoza wrappers
to servechili oil

The filling is forgiving. If it looks loose, add another spoon of cornstarch and rest it for ten minutes.

Method

1

Grate the garlic and ginger. Slice the green onions, keeping the white and green parts apart.

2

In a bowl, combine the pork, garlic, ginger, onion whites, soy sauce, oyster sauce, cornstarch, water, salt and white pepper.

2 min
3

Mix the filling until it is sticky and well combined. Fold it over itself a few times rather than stirring in circles.

4

Place a wet gyoza wrapper in the bottom of a ramekin. Add a spoon of filling, then press a second wrapper on top. Repeat to build the layers.

8 min
5

Steam over rolling water for 12 minutes. The wrapper should turn translucent and pull away from the ramekin cleanly.

12 min
6

Turn out onto a plate, spoon over the chili oil and finish with the green onion tops.

From the video

“Do not skip the wet wrapper. The water is what steams the layer from the inside and keeps it from cracking.”
