Shrimp Ramen, Soft Egg

No. 0420 / Japanese / saved today

No. 0420 / Japanese / Weeknight

Shrimp Ramen,
Soft Egg

25 min cook4 servingsNoodlesSeafood

Ingredients

4
400 gshrimp, peeled
4eggs
2 packsramen noodles
1 Lchicken stock
2 tbspwhite miso
1 tbspsoy sauce
20 gginger
2 clovesgarlic
2spring onions
1 tspchili oil

Keep the egg yolks just set. Six and a half minutes from boiling, then straight into cold water.

Method

1

Boil the eggs for six and a half minutes, then chill them in cold water and peel.

6.5 min
2

Warm the stock with the miso, soy, grated ginger and garlic. Keep it just below a simmer.

5 min
3

Sear the shrimp in a hot pan with a little oil until pink and opaque, about two minutes a side.

4 min
4

Cook the noodles separately so the broth stays clear. Drain and divide between four bowls.

3 min
5

Ladle the broth over the noodles, add the shrimp, halve the eggs and lay them on top.

6

Finish with spring onion, chili oil and a pinch of salt. Serve immediately.

From the video

“Do not let the broth boil after the miso goes in. It turns grainy and loses the sweetness.”
