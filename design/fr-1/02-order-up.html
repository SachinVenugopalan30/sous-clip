Steamed Pork Dumplings
(Layered Wrapper Style)

20 min cook8 min prep4 servingsChinese / Steamed

Ingredients / 12

ground pork400 g
garlic, grated4 cloves
ginger, grated1 tbsp
green onions3
light soy sauce2 tbsp
oyster sauce1 tbsp
cornstarch1 tbsp
water2 tbsp
salt1 tsp
white pepper½ tsp
gyoza wrappers24
chili oilto serve

Method / 9

1
Grate the garlic and ginger. Slice the green onions, keeping the white and green parts apart.
2
Combine the pork, garlic, ginger, onion whites, soy, oyster sauce, cornstarch, water, salt and white pepper.2 min
3
Mix the filling until it is sticky and well combined. Fold it over itself rather than stirring in circles.
4
Place a wet gyoza wrapper in a ramekin. Add a spoon of filling, press a second wrapper on top, and repeat to build the layers.8 min
5
Steam over rolling water for 12 minutes, until the wrapper turns translucent and pulls away cleanly.12 min
6
Turn out onto a plate, spoon over the chili oil and finish with the green onion tops.
From the video
“Do not skip the wet wrapper. The water is what steams the layer from the inside and keeps it from cracking.”
